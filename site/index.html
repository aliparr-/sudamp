01 How it works

A three-step program. We counted.

  1. 1
    Deep HouseHouseTechnoDrum & BassLo-fiAmbient

    Pick a genre.

    Deep House, Techno, Drum & Bass, Lo-fi… one click on the big glowing button. No playlists to build, no algorithm to train, no account to make.

  2. 2
    ~2s
    YouTubeSoundCloudMixcloudRadio

    Music plays.

    A random mix from a hand-picked list starts in about two seconds, from YouTube, SoundCloud, Mixcloud or internet radio. Just the audio. Not feeling it? Hit Next.

  3. 3
    NumbersFileEditFri 9:41
    Q3 budget FINAL final (2).numbers

    Back to work.

    sudamp waits quietly in your menu bar while you do the actual job. Open it tomorrow and it carries on where you left off.

02 Visualisers

Twenty-eight tiny shows. Every one of them listens.

The artwork dances to whatever's playing. Pick a favourite, or set it to Rotate and sudamp moves through them all, choosing scenes that suit the genre: a campfire for lo-fi, a tunnel for techno, a game of rally for drum & bass.

FX Add a finish

Rewind to 1987.

Lay an effect over any scene. VHS brings the rental-shop tape: colour bleed, scanlines, a tracking band and the deck's PLAY sign. Kaleidoscope folds the scene into twelve mirrored wedges and slowly turns it.

Classic

sudamp The Sudden Amp

A free, open-source music player for macOS. Made in T3 Code.

sudamp is an unofficial client: every stream belongs to its creator or broadcaster. It isn't affiliated with YouTube, SoundCloud, Mixcloud or Winamp. Mix titles on this page are made up; the music on this page is synthesised in your browser. AndAmp skins by Mattix, CC BY 4.0.
