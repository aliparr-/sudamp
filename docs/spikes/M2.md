# M2: walking skeleton app (plain window)

**Verdict: GO** (2026-10-22, macOS 27.0.1, M5 Pro, UK residential broadband, `make app` debug bundle, yt-dlp
2026.10.21 from the feed's pin). Open `build/sudamp.app`, pick **Deep House** in a plain dropdown, and music starts:
radio in 0.6–1.5 s on a fresh profile while yt-dlp downloads, any source once it is installed. Every PLAN M2 gate below
passed, with one substitution: `sudamp-dev wait` is written (`Sources/sudamp-dev/Commands/Wait.swift`) but its dispatch
line belongs in `sudamp-dev/main.swift`, which this agent doesn't own, so gate (d) polled `state.json` with an
equivalent `jq` loop. All launches were muted (`--mute`, plus the debug-only `/tmp/sudamp-force-mute` guard); no
audible sound was played. YouTube budget: **1 of 15** resolves used; no bot check, `LOGIN_REQUIRED` or 429.

## What was built

- **Composition root** (`Sources/Shell`): `SudampApp` (modes: `--selftest`, `--engine-check`, `--skin-preview`
  unchanged; default and `--plain` open the plain window), `AppDelegate` (lifecycle, URL events, Dock, the quit
  path), `AppContext` (profile, feed, `PlaybackController.live`, settings, instance socket, one command router for
  every surface).
- **PlainWindow** (`PlainWindowController`): NSPopUpButton with every genre (disabled with the reason when nothing
  can play, as a menu-item subtitle) plus Surprise Me; Previous / Play-Pause / Next with SF Symbols; volume slider
  (remembered in `<profile>/settings.json`); title line (`CREATOR - TITLE`, or the ICY title for radio), detail line
  (`YT`/`SC`/`MC`/`FM` plus `LIVE` or `12:34 / 1:58:20`), status line (`PLAYING · YouTube · TTFA 1821 ms`,
  `TUNING…`, `SKIPPING - UNAVAILABLE`, `OFFLINE`, `CAN'T REACH STREAMS - CLICK TO RETRY`, `AUDIO ENGINE ERROR`,
  `MUTED`), an engine line that appears only while it matters (`PREPARING STREAM ENGINE 63%`, `ENGINE DOWNLOAD
  FAILED - RADIO ONLY`, `STREAM ENGINE NEEDS 180 MB FREE - RADIO ONLY`) and a Retry button for failures. Text comes
  from the unit-tested `Playback.PlainDisplay`. Hovering a genre for 300 ms pre-resolves its next pick.
- **First launch**: nothing plays; the dropdown opens once by itself (`settings.json` `genreMenuShown`). The engine
  installs in the background (`ensureEngine`), radio-first picks meanwhile. Failed installs retry with backoff
  15 s → 1 min → 5 min → 15 min → 30 min, at once when the network comes back after `unreachable`, or on Retry; the
  failure stays on screen until a retry's bytes flow. `state.json` `.engine.download` is `unreachable`, `noSpace` or
  the install phase, plus `.engine.retryAt` and `.engine.attempts`.
- **Returning launch**: `Playback.ResumePolicy` (auto-resume on by default; only if something was playing at quit):
  a VOD from its saved position if quit under 6 h ago with over 5 min left, live/radio at the live edge, else the
  genre's next bag item; without the engine only radio replays. `PlaybackController.resume(_:)` applies it behind
  the 1.5 s launch fade (`PlayerEngine.fadeIn` → the existing `RenderBridge.fadeIn`; no Audio change needed) and sets
  `state.json` `.resumed` (now "this launch auto-resumed") and `.resumePlan`. A paused/stopped session stays quiet and
  the window offers `Press Play for <genre>`.
- **Shell basics**: App menu (About with the unofficial-client note and yt-dlp credit, Hide, Quit), Genre menu
  (⌘1–⌘9, ⌘0 Surprise Me, current genre ticked), Controls (Winamp keys X/C/V/B/Z, ⌘↑/⌘↓ volume, Retry), Window;
  status item (now playing, genres, Play/Pause, Next, Show, Quit); Dock menu (genres, Play/Pause, Next); one genre
  menu builder over `Playback.GenreMenuModel`. `sudamp://genre/<id> | surprise | toggle | play | pause | stop | next
  | previous | quit` (`Core.RemoteCommand`). Now Playing and media keys (`NowPlayingController`): `playbackState` on
  every phase change (tuning/buffering/offline → interrupted), `IsLiveStream`, `ExcludeFromSuggestions`, next = random
  pick, previous = history, seeking disabled; what was published is in `state.json` `.nowPlaying`.
- **Single instance**: the `InstanceLock` is taken before anything starts; a second launch sends `{arguments, urls}`
  to `<profile>/instance.sock` (`Core.InstanceServer`/`InstanceClient`: one JSON line, `ok` reply, `0600` socket,
  `getpeereid` same-user check, `SO_NOSIGPIPE`, 3 s connect retry while the instance starts) and exits 0; it exits 75
  if nobody answers. `--automation` never forwards (busy profile → 75).
- **Quit path**: ⌘Q, window close, `sudamp://quit`, SIGTERM and SIGINT all go through `AppDelegate.quit` →
  `applicationShouldTerminate` → `.terminateLater`: clear Now Playing, flush session.json/library.json/settings.json
  first, close the socket, then `PlaybackController.shutdown()` (5 ms fade, engine shutdown order, flush again,
  state.json), release the lock, exit 0. A 1.8 s watchdog `_exit(0)`s a wedged teardown (never fired in the gates).
- **Agent hooks**: `--automation <script.json>` (`Core.AutomationScript`: `selectGenre`, `surpriseMe`,
  `press{Play,Pause,PlayPause,Next,Previous,Stop,Retry}`, `waitFor` state(s), `waitForEngine`, `sleep`, `dumpState`,
  `openURL`, `setVolume`, `snapshot` (window PNG, works off screen); `--skips N [--skip-dwell S]`), driving the
  window's own controls; prints one JSON document (state.json's keys at the top level plus `automation`: per-step
  results, starts with TTFA, p50/p90) and exits 0/1. Automation runs as an accessory app (no Dock icon, no focus
  steal, no status item, no Now Playing). `--mute`/`SUDAMP_MUTE=1` force mixer volume 0 and zero the bridge output.
  Debug only: `--feed <path>`, `--source k[,k…]`, `SUDAMP_FAULT=engine:unreachable | engine:abort-at=<pct> |
  disk:full` (`Resolver.EngineFaults`, wrapping the release source and the free-space probe), the
  `/tmp/sudamp-force-mute` sentinel. Fixtures: `Fixtures/automation/{northstar-plain, engine-unreachable,
  engine-abort, engine-ready, engine-nospace}.json`.

## Findings

- **`NSApp.terminate` from a main-actor job wedges `.terminateLater`.** SIGTERM, URL and forwarded quits run inside a
  Swift main-actor job (a main-queue block); `.terminateLater` then spins a nested run loop that can't drain the
  main queue from inside that block, so the shutdown Task never started and the watchdog fired at 1.8 s. Fix:
  `NSApp.perform(#selector(terminate(_:)), afterDelay: 0, inModes: [.common])`, after cancelling any menu tracking
  (the first-launch dropdown runs its own run-loop mode and swallowed a default-mode perform). M5 should keep this.
- **Surprise Me could override a later pick.** It chooses its genre after an actor hop; a pick, Next or URL in that
  window was then replaced by the surprise genre (seen in an automation script). The controller now drops a Surprise
  Me that finishes after a later user action (test proven to fail without the guard), and `--automation` waits for
  its pick to start.
- **Radio TTFA depends on the station, not on the concurrent 54 MB engine download.** Deep House, fresh profiles:
  1.FM 0.58–0.65 s, Deep Radio 1.07–1.41 s, Technolovers 1.16–1.90 s (its URL redirects to another host: a second TLS
  handshake). With and without the download (`engine:unreachable`) the numbers overlap. The 2 s first-launch target
  holds with margin only for direct station URLs; M7's `catalog-tool probe` could prefer them, or fast start could
  prefer the fastest-measured station.
- **The first pick isn't always radio once the engine is ready**: fast start prefers a ready item among the next 3
  in the bag, then the feed's rank; in gate (b) the next 3 held no radio, so a cold SoundCloud pick (2.07 s) opened.
- **`state.json` is only rewritten on snapshot changes**, so while paused `.player.outputRunning` can be stale (the
  engine had stopped after 30 s; CPU confirmed). `dumpState`/`writeDebugStateNow` refresh it.
- **`open -a build/sudamp.app` (relative) fails on macOS 27** ("Unable to find application named"); PLAN's gate
  commands need `open -a "$PWD/build/sudamp.app"`.

## Gaps and follow-ups

- `sudamp-dev wait`: needs one dispatch line in `sudamp-dev/main.swift` (requested); its matcher is unit-tested.
- Not terminal-verifiable here: media keys and Control Center, the status item and Dock menus, ⌘Q from the menu
  and closing the window (same quit path as `sudamp://quit`, entered from AppKit instead of a job) — in the owner's
  checklist (`docs/feedback/M2.md`).
- DESIGN §3's yt-dlp `--version` warm-up on a returning launch is not done yet.
- The "over 5 min left" rule uses the feed's `durationSec` (`LastSession` has no mpv duration; Picker isn't this
  agent's); an unknown duration resumes.
- Shell has no test target; pure logic lives in Core and Playback so it is tested. A `ShellTests` target would let
  M5 test AppKit glue.

## Reproduce

```
make app
LSREG=/System/Library/Frameworks/CoreServices.framework/Frameworks/LaunchServices.framework/Support/lsregister
$LSREG -f build/sudamp.app
A=build/sudamp.app/Contents/MacOS/sudamp; APP="$PWD/build/sudamp.app"
P=$(mktemp -d)
$A --plain --mute --profile "$P" --automation Fixtures/automation/northstar-plain.json --json \
  | jq -e '.state=="playing" and .genre=="deep-house" and .source=="radio" and .ttfaMs<=2000'          # (a)
.build/debug/sudamp-dev engine install --profile "$P"
$A --plain --mute --profile "$P" --automation Fixtures/automation/northstar-plain.json --skips 5 --json \
  | jq -e '.automation.nonRadioStarts>=1 and (.automation.starts|length)>=5 and .automation.ttfaP90Ms<=3500'  # (b)
Q=$(mktemp -d); SUDAMP_FAULT=engine:unreachable $A --plain --mute --profile "$Q" \
  --automation Fixtures/automation/engine-unreachable.json --json | jq -e '.source=="radio" and .engine.download=="unreachable"'  # (c)
Q=$(mktemp -d); SUDAMP_FAULT=disk:full $A --plain --mute --profile "$Q" \
  --automation Fixtures/automation/engine-nospace.json --json | jq -e '.engine.download=="noSpace"'
Q=$(mktemp -d); SUDAMP_FAULT=engine:abort-at=50 $A --plain --mute --profile "$Q" --automation Fixtures/automation/engine-abort.json --json >/dev/null
$A --plain --mute --profile "$Q" --automation Fixtures/automation/engine-ready.json --json | jq -c .engine
grep engine.download.resume "$Q/Logs/metrics.jsonl"
D=$(mktemp -d); open -a "$APP" --args --plain --mute --profile "$D"; open -a "$APP" 'sudamp://genre/deep-house'  # (d)
until jq -e '.state=="playing"' "$D/debug/state.json" >/dev/null 2>&1; do sleep 0.1; done   # = sudamp-dev wait --state playing
jq -e '.nowPlaying.playbackState=="playing"' "$D/debug/state.json"
$A --profile "$D" 'sudamp://next'; echo $?; pgrep -x sudamp | wc -l
open -a "$APP" 'sudamp://quit'                                                                            # (e)
open -a "$APP" --args --plain --mute --profile "$D"; jq '{resumed, genre}' "$D/debug/state.json"
kill -TERM $(pgrep -x sudamp)
plutil -extract CFBundleURLTypes.0.CFBundleURLSchemes.0 raw build/sudamp.app/Contents/Info.plist        # (f)
```
