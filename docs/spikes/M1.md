# M1: thin slice, headless (plus M3's resolver and playback parts)

**Verdict: GO for the M1 north star** (2026-10-22, macOS 27.0.1, M5 Pro, residential UK broadband, yt-dlp 2026.10.21
pinned from the bundled feed). `sudamp-dev play --genre deep-house` plays real feed streams end to end: feed →
Picker → Resolver → mpv → pull AO → AVAudioEngine, through `PlaybackController`. Every gate below passed except one
row: cold YouTube `/live` TTFA missed DESIGN §2 by 115 ms at p50, n = 3, because yt-dlp's `/live` resolve takes most
of the 3 s (see Findings). One crash bug outside this agent's files (MPVClient's event handler) was found and
worked around here; the fix is requested from the audio agent.

Code: `Sources/Playback/*` (PlaybackController, MPVPlayerEngine, LoadPlan, scheduler, models),
`Sources/Resolver/{TokenBucket,ResolutionCache,TierAdaptation,StreamResolver,MediaProbe,EngineProviding,
ResolverSettings+Feed}.swift`, `Sources/Core/{InstanceLock,NetworkPath,SystemProxy,SignalWatcher}.swift`, three new
`SessionMachine` events (`playStream`, `resolveDeferred`, `reloadCurrent`), and `sudamp-dev play | ttfa`; `engine
install` now honours the feed's `pin`/`block`, and `resolve` uses the feed's engine strings and the system proxy.
Tests: 93 new Swift Testing tests (Core 14, Resolver 35, Picker 4 plus the extended transition matrix, Playback 40).
The whole package has 561 tests, green in 6 consecutive `--parallel` runs; `scripts/count-escapes.sh` reports 3
escapes, 0 unjustified, none new; and `scripts/check-warnings.sh` passes on a full build log (no new warnings).

## Findings

- **MPVClient's event handler overflows the event thread (MPV module, not ours).** The first 120 s gate run died
  with SIGBUS in `sudamp.mpv.events`: `EventPump.deliver` calls `handler.withLock { $0 }?(event)`. On Swift 6.4,
  reading an optional closure out of a `Mutex` that way adds **2 reabstraction-thunk frames per call, even at `-O`**.
  A 12-line repro grows by exactly 40,000 frames per 20,000 calls. Thread's 512 KB stack overflows after about
  100 s of playback. Every `MPVClient(eventHandler:)` user is affected, including `EngineHarness` (so `soak` will
  crash). Workaround here: `MPVPlayerEngine` passes no handler and consumes `mpv.events` on a detached task, which
  also arms TTFA. 150 s and 120 s runs after the fix were clean. The fix belongs in MPVClient (see the report's
  requested changes).
- **YouTube `/live` cold TTFA is about 3 s, mostly yt-dlp.** The resolves took 2199, 2278 and 3184 ms (R1's p50 was
  2368 ms), and the HLS open about 0.4–0.8 s on top. Prefetch hides it for every pick after the first (the
  prefetched `/live` start in the play gate took 462 ms), and fast-start ranks YouTube last for first picks. No
  YouTube flags were tried (no evidence they help, and each try costs budget).
- **HLS opens are the slow part of prefetched SoundCloud/Mixcloud picks (0.9–2.5 s).** The mpv `-v` log of a saved
  SoundCloud resolution shows FFmpeg's HLS demuxer fetching `init.mp4`, `data000.m4s` and `data001.m4s` **twice**
  before first audio. The second round follows mpv's `refresh track 0`, the refresh seek it does when the audio
  track is selected after probing. That's about 0.5–1 s per HLS start. A YouTube progressive start, by contrast,
  takes 0.1–0.25 s. Not fixed in M1 (needs mpv/FFmpeg option work); the play gate's prefetched p50 passed thanks
  to the YouTube and `/live` picks.
- **Prefetching radio saves nothing**: a prefetched radio pick still opens in about 1.35 s. It costs nothing either.
- **Token-bucket maths under a broken tier 1.** Every pick costs 2 tokens (tier 1, then tier 2) until the 24 h
  preference starts, so a 10 s prefetch right after a pick can run out of tokens for its own step-up. That's
  DESIGN §6.3's shared bucket working as specified. The M3 tier check therefore ran with `--prefetch=false` and
  picks 30 s apart. PLAN M3's `--next-every 15 --seconds 60` variant would hit this.
- **`available_at`** was always "now" (or absent) in these runs; the wait and deferral paths are covered by unit
  tests only.
- **Default timer leeway**: `Task.sleep(for: .seconds(45))` slept 47.8 s in a CLI process, so the scheduler now
  passes an explicit 50 ms tolerance.

## Behaviour as built

- **PlaybackController** (`@MainActor`) is the effect executor around `SessionMachine`:
  - **Pick**: prefetched pick first, if still eligible. `PickContext` gets tokens, ready resolutions and open
    sources from the resolver, plus bans, the engine state and the feed revision.
  - **Resolve**: `.userPick`; 15 s timeout; `available_at` waits up to 3 s, else defers (not a failure; more than 8
    deferrals in a row count as one).
  - **Load**:
    - radio needs no resolve and goes through the DNS rule first;
    - `LoadPlan` maps a `ResolvedStream` to mpv's per-file options: headers, user agent, title, `request_size` for
      progressive, reconnects, and the system proxy as `http-proxy`;
    - 20 s first-audio timeout.
  - **TTFA**: from the user action (or the auto-advance) to the C-side stamp. Logged as `ttfa {ms, attemptMs,
    source, streamId, tier, prefetched, fromCache, trigger, genre}`.
  - **Failures**: classified into Picker health. 5 in a row → `failed`.
  - **Prefetch**: depth 1, 10 s after first audio; progressive YouTube gets the 64 KB range probe, where a 403
    evicts the resolution and counts as an engine failure.
  - **Pause and resume**: host first, then mpv, and the reverse to resume (`TransportOrder`, unit-tested). Live
    streams and radio paused over 30 s reload at the live edge; YouTube `/live` re-resolves only when its URL has
    expired.
  - **Idle**: the audio engine stops after 30 s paused or idle.
  - **Network**: `NWPathMonitor` drives `networkLost`/`networkRestored`; YouTube resolutions are dropped on a
    route change.
  - **Guards**: startup, first file and every read. A failure shows AUDIO ENGINE ERROR, refuses playback and
    asserts in debug.
  - **Persistence**: session.json and library.json debounced 2 s and atomic, flushed on `shutdown()`;
    `debug/state.json` debounced 250 ms.
- **StreamResolver** (actor) composes the shared token bucket, the cache, `available_at`, the JS ladder with its
  persisted 24 h adaptation (`<profile>/resolver.json`), the bot-check pause, the per-resolve proxy read and the
  prefetch probe over `ResolverService`. Concurrent requests for one stream share one yt-dlp run; the last waiter
  to cancel kills it.
- **Engine readiness**: `EngineStore.readiness()` re-hashes `tree.json`. Radio only while the engine isn't ready.
  `ensureEngine()` installs in the background honouring the feed's `pin`/`block`, with progress in the snapshot.

## Reproduce

```
P=/tmp/sudamp-m1; mkdir -p "$P"
swift build --scratch-path .build-playback --product sudamp-dev; D=.build-playback/debug/sudamp-dev
$D engine install --profile "$P"
$D play --genre deep-house --profile "$P" --seconds 120 --next-every 20 --json
$D ttfa --source radio,radio-http,soundcloud,mixcloud,youtube,youtube-live -n 3 --profile "$P" --json
$D play --genre deep-house --profile "$P" --no-engine --seconds 62 --next-every 12 --json
$D play --genre lo-fi --profile "$P" --seconds 12 & sleep 3; $D play --genre lo-fi --profile "$P"   # exit 75
$D play --genre deep-house --profile "$P" --source radio --pause-at 8,70 --pause-for 45,10 --seconds 90 --json
python3 -I /tmp/sudamp-proxy/proxy.py 8899 /tmp/sudamp-proxy/proxy.log &      # logging CONNECT proxy
SUDAMP_PROXY=http://127.0.0.1:8899 $D play --genre deep-house --profile "$P" --source radio --seconds 8
rm -f "$P/resolver.json"
SUDAMP_FAULT=youtube:jsless-fail $D play --genre deep-house --profile "$P" --source youtube --seconds 65 \
    --next-every 30 --prefetch=false --json
SUDAMP_CLOCK_OFFSET=3600 $D play --genre deep-house --profile "$P" --source youtube --seconds 7 --prefetch=false --json
jq -c 'select(.event=="ttfa" or .event=="liveReload" or .event=="tier.adapt")' "$P/Logs/metrics.jsonl"
```
