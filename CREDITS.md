# Credits

**sudamp** (The Sudden Amp) is a free, open-source radio-style player for macOS: pick a genre and a random curated
stream plays, in a player that wears classic Winamp skins.

sudamp is an **unofficial** client. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube, SoundCloud, Mixcloud, any radio
station, or Winamp. Every mix, live set and station belongs to its creator or broadcaster; sudamp plays streams
without downloading them, shows the creator in every title, and "Copy Link" always points back to the original page.

This file is the human-readable list. The licence notices that must travel with the app, generated and checked by
`scripts/check-notices.sh`, are in [`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md); the file-by-file record of
everything ported or adapted is [`PROVENANCE.md`](PROVENANCE.md).

## The people whose streams you hear

The curated catalog (`catalog/feed.json`) credits the DJ, label, channel or station of every entry, and the app shows
that credit in the marquee and the playlist. Internet radio stations are listed with their homepages; many were
found through [radio-browser.info](https://www.radio-browser.info).

## The engine

- **[mpv](https://mpv.io)** (LGPL-2.1-or-later) and **[FFmpeg](https://ffmpeg.org)** (LGPL-3.0-or-later build) decode
  every stream, together with the libraries listed in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`, prebuilt by
  **[MPVKit](https://github.com/mpvkit/MPVKit)** 1.0.0.
- **[yt-dlp](https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp)** (Unlicense) finds the audio for YouTube, SoundCloud and Mixcloud
  streams. sudamp downloads it from yt-dlp's official releases on first launch; it is never bundled or redistributed.
- **[QuickJS-NG](https://github.com/quickjs-ng/quickjs)** (MIT) is bundled as `qjs`, yt-dlp's fallback JavaScript
  runtime.

## Skins

- **AndAmp Dark** and **AndAmp Light** by **Mattix** (<https://mattix.nl>), made for
  [AndAmp](https://github.com/mattijsf/andamp), ship unmodified under the
  [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) licence. The AndAmp name and icon
  are not licensed; they are named here only to credit the artist.
- **sudamp Dark**, the default skin, is original art generated by sudamp's `skingen` (GPL-3.0-or-later), using
  drawing routines adapted from Bitamp. It contains no Nullsoft or Bitamp artwork.
- The **[Winamp Skin Museum](https://skins.webamp.org)** by Jordan Eldredge is where "Get more skins…" takes you.
  Skins you add are yours to keep locally; sudamp never redistributes them.
