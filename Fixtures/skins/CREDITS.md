# Skins in this folder

| File | What | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| `sudamp-dark.wsz` | sudamp's original default skin, generated by `skingen` (`Sources/SkinKit/Generator`); must stay byte-identical to the generator's output (`skingen --check Fixtures/skins/sudamp-dark.wsz`) | GPL-3.0-or-later (part of sudamp) |
| `AndAmp-Dark.wsz`, `AndAmp-Light.wsz`, `AndAmp-Spot.wsz` | AndAmp Dark, AndAmp Light and AndAmp Spot by **Mattix** (<https://mattix.nl>), made for [AndAmp](https://github.com/mattijsf/andamp); unmodified copies of `skin/dist/AndAmp Dark.wsz`, `skin/dist/AndAmp Light.wsz` and `skin/dist/AndAmp Spot.wsz` at commit `1458304acd4e70ecfb357a5356bd3a571bac170e` (SHA-256 `d4d9cf69…7c58`, `3aeeaeda…7f27db` and `4a06a9c7…2a94fb`) | [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) |
| `museum.json` | The 100 [Winamp Skin Museum](https://skins.webamp.org) skins bundled in the app's Skins ▸ Skin Museum submenu: each one's MD5, museum file name and menu name. The skins are the `make golden` corpus (`scripts/golden-fetch.sh`: complete, `nsfw: false`), with names tidied from the file names by hand. `scripts/fetch-museum-skins.sh` copies them into `Contents/Resources/Skins/Museum` at `make app` time from the golden cache, downloading any that are missing and checking each against its MD5; the `.wsz` files are never committed | List: factual. Skins: each its author's; no licence stated (see below) |
| `webamp-fixtures/` | Webamp's parser test fixtures and region snapshot (see its README) | MIT © 2015 Jordan Eldredge |

AndAmp's README: "The bundled skins are artwork. AndAmp Dark, Light and Spot (the `.wsz` files in `skin/dist/` and the
art inside them) are licensed under CC BY 4.0." The Python build that draws them is GPL and is not used here. The
"Andamp" name and icon are not licensed; sudamp only names the skins to credit them.

The museum skins carry no licence: neither the museum nor the Internet Archive records rights, and each skin is its
author's work. Bundling them was the owner's decision on 2026-10-23, against the research recommendation
(`docs/research/skins.md`, DESIGN §8.3); keeping the files out of this public repository limits that to the built
app. Some skins show third-party trademarks, characters or photographs. Each is credited by its museum file name in
the Skins menu tooltip and the About window. To drop one, remove its line from `museum.json`.

Zip bombs and fuzzed archives are generated at test time (`ZipBomb.make`, `SkinFuzzer`); none are committed.
